📖 Step by step

  1. Download and install App Shelf. Tap the Download button above and open the file when it finishes. If Android blocks it, allow installs from your browser, go back, and tap the file again.
  2. Open App Shelf and tap + Add app.
  3. Type an app name, for example OTP MANAGER. Then tap the Source box.
  4. Choose "GitHub or web link (saved on device)". Don't choose "Upload or paste HTML".
  5. Paste the link into the Link box. Tap and hold the box, then tap Paste. Or use the Copy link button above. Then tap Save.
  6. Tap Open on the OTP MANAGER card. Sign in with the account you were given.
Opening it offline: after the first save, the page opens from the copy on your phone, even without internet. Signing in needs internet.
Updates: We update our app and site for any bug fixes. This page will be updated automatically for a new version.

❓ Troubleshooting

  • Android says the app is unsafe: tap More details, then Install anyway. This happens with apps installed outside the Play Store.
  • The download is blocked: allow downloads from your browser, then tap Download again.
  • The app shows a blank page: check your internet connection, then tap Update.
  • Sign-in fails: check your email and password with the owner. Each account must be created by the owner.
